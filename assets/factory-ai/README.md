Homepage hero prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium full-width website landing-page hero. Input image: edit target and strict invariant reference. Regenerate this same Kudamalana Tea Factory aerial image with visibly higher photographic clarity, micro-detail, and natural vibrance. Preserve the exact 16:9 crop, drone camera position, perspective, factory footprint, roof geometry, solar-panel arrangement, chimney, outbuildings, paths, vehicles, people positions, trees, palms, fields, and distant hills. Keep the factory in the same lower-center placement and preserve useful darker visual space on the left for white website text. Make solar panels, corrugated metal, windows, roads, and foliage clean and sharply resolved with realistic textures and straight edges. Use clear warm tropical daylight, a fresh blue sky, richer separation among emerald, forest, and yellow-green foliage, restrained golden highlights, clean atmospheric depth, and balanced contrast. Aim for premium architectural drone photography and crisp 4K-like detail. Avoid structure changes, invented buildings, extra objects, changed viewpoints, fake signage, painterly foliage, plastic textures, halos, crunchy HDR, excessive saturation, haze, blur, noise, and compression artifacts. No text, no logo, no watermark.

Each original was supplied separately as the edit target using this same prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Input image: edit target and strict composition/architecture reference. Recreate this exact Kudamalana tea factory photograph as a new high-resolution photorealistic architectural photograph, landscape 16:9. Preserve the exact viewpoint, framing, building footprints, roof slopes, solar-panel layout, windows, outbuildings, chimney, roads, vehicles, people positions, palms and surrounding terrain. Improve photographic clarity, clean naturally resolved foliage, straight crisp architectural edges, realistic solar-cell textures, balanced exposure, subtle warm sunlight, rich but believable tropical greens and clear atmospheric depth. Preserve the real utilitarian factory's weathering. No new buildings or landscaping, no redesign, no invented text, no oversharpening halos, no crunchy HDR, no painterly or plastic foliage. Match the original geometry as closely as possible; make a polished realistic reconstruction rather than merely increasing saturation. Output one image only, preferably 2560x1440 or higher.

Actual output dimensions are recorded in `src/data/imageManifest.json`; the
requested size is not a guarantee of the generator's output. These are AI
reconstructions, not documentary photographs or evidence of specific features.
This provenance note is maintained for internal asset tracking; generation
details are not displayed in public-facing site copy. Main composition follows
the references, but generated fine details can vary. Layout-only hero framing,
including the current rightward crop, is controlled in `src/styles.css` and does
not require regenerating the master image.
